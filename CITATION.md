# Citation Information

Guidance on citing Alex Liu's "Stored, Not Integrated: A Pre-Treatment Predictor and Three Controls for Knowledge Injection."

**For citing the latest version**, use the concept DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.21865285](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21865285). It always resolves to the most recent release.
